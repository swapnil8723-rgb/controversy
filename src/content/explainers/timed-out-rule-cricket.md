## The rule

- **What counts:** the incoming batter must be **ready to receive the ball**, not just on the field or walking to the crease.
- **How long:** three minutes under MCC's Laws. ICC events can set their own playing conditions, and the 2023 Men's World Cup used **two minutes**.
- **Who decides:** the fielding side must appeal. The umpires then decide, with the TV umpire tracking the time at ICC events.

## The Mathews case in 30 seconds

Angelo Mathews's helmet strap broke as he got ready to face. Bangladesh appealed and he was given out. The ICC's fourth umpire said he was not ready within two minutes even before the strap problem. MCC later said the umpires were right, and that he could have avoided it by asking them for time. [Full timeline](/cricket/controversies/angelo-mathews-timed-out/).

## Can a batter avoid it?

Yes. MCC's point is that the umpires can allow time if they are told about a genuine problem, such as broken equipment, before the limit runs out.
