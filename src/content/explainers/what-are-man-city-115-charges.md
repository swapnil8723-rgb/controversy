## Why "115"?

The league did not allege 115 separate incidents. It listed the rules it said City broke in each season, and each rule in each season counts as one alleged breach. That is how six groups of conduct add up to 115 charges.

## The six groups

| Group | What the league alleged City failed to do | Seasons |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Financial information | Provide "accurate financial information that gives a true and fair view of the club's financial position" | 2009/10 to 2017/18 |
| 2. Manager pay | Include "full details of manager remuneration" in its contracts with its manager | 2009/10 to 2012/13 |
| 3. Player pay | Include "full details of player remuneration" in its contracts with its players | 2010/11 to 2015/16 |
| 4. UEFA rules | Comply with UEFA's club licensing and Financial Fair Play regulations | 2013/14 to 2017/18 |
| 5. Profitability and sustainability | Comply with the league's own profitability and sustainability rules | 2015/16 to 2017/18 |
| 6. Co-operation | "Cooperate with, and assist, the Premier League in its investigations" | December 2018 onwards |

Source: the Premier League's statement of 6 February 2023.

## What the commission decided

On 29 September 2026 the league said the independent commission found City guilty of all the charges related to its financial rules, over the nine seasons from 2009/10 to 2017/18, and upheld most of the co-operation charges. Sanctions will be set at a separate hearing, and City has appealed. The full timeline is on the [Man City 115 charges record](/football/controversies/man-city-115-charges/).
