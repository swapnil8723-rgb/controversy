This page explains a court ruling. It is not legal advice.

## Defamation in one paragraph

Broadly, defamation is a **false statement of fact** that damages someone's reputation. **Opinion**, meaning statements a reasonable audience would not take as provable fact, is generally protected in the US. So a key question in many cases is whether the words would be understood as fact or as opinion.

## What the judge decided

US District Judge Jeannette Vargas dismissed Drake's suit against Universal Music Group in October 2025. She ruled that "the allegedly defamatory statements in Not Like Us are nonactionable opinion", reasoning that the context of a heated rap battle signals opinion to a reasonable listener.

## What Drake's appeal argues

Drake's lawyers say the decision created an "unprecedented and overbroad categorical rule that statements in rap diss tracks can never constitute statements of fact". The Second Circuit will decide whether the judge was right. [Full timeline](/entertainment/controversies/drake-umg-not-like-us-lawsuit/).

## Does this apply in India, the UK or Australia?

Not directly. This is a US federal case, and defamation law differs from country to country. A ruling in New York does not decide how courts elsewhere treat song lyrics.
