## What has not been decided

The commission's decision does not include a punishment. The league says sanctions will be set at a separate hearing, and City's appeal goes to an independent Appeal Board. Until both finish, any talk of points deductions, fines or relegation is speculation, and this page will not report it as fact.

## What "115 charges" means

The number counts individual alleged rule breaches season by season, not 115 separate incidents. The league grouped them into the six categories listed in the February 2023 entry above.
